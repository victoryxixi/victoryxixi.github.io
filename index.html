I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Building Environment and Energy Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, advised by Dr Anthony Chun Yin Yuen. I received my M.Sc. from The University of Hong Kong in 2024 and my B.Sc. from Shanghai University in 2023. My research interests lie in lithium-ion battery safety, thermal management, and physics-informed machine learning.

Research

I combine computational fluid dynamics, thermal resistance networks and coupled multiphysics models to study battery cooling, thermal runaway propagation and early warning.
