BATTERY SAFETY · THERMAL SCIENCE · AI

Hi, I’m Yongxi Wu.

Understanding batteries through physics.
Building safer energy systems with intelligent models.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Building Environment and Energy Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, supervised by Dr Anthony Chun Yin Yuen and Dr Xinyan Huang.

My research focuses on lithium-ion battery thermal management and safety, combining computational fluid dynamics, coupled multiphysics modelling, and machine learning. I study how heat moves through batteries, how thermal runaway propagates, and how physical models can support earlier fault identification.

I received my M.Sc. from The University of Hong Kong in 2024 and my B.Sc. from Shanghai University in 2023.

Research interests

Thermal runaway & early warning

Battery thermal management

Immersion cooling, phase-change materials, thermal barriers, and multi-objective optimisation of cooling systems.

Physics-informed intelligence
